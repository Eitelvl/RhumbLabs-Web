# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last update:** November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RhumbLabs, RUT 19.524.712-9 (the "Company"), collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal data of users of the Pogo (the "Application"). This Privacy Policy should be read in conjunction with the Terms and Conditions of Use of the Application.

By using the Application, the User agrees to the practices described in this document. This policy is currently governed by Chilean Law N° 19.628 on the Protection of Privacy and Law N° 19.496 on Consumer Protection. Law N° 21.719 on the Protection and Processing of Personal Data will take effect on December 1, 2026.

The Company is the data controller responsible for the personal data processed through the Application and can be contacted at: support@rhumblabs.com By using the Application, the User acknowledges having read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy forms an integral part of the Terms and Conditions of Use of the Application.

## 1. Data We Collect

The Application collects only the data necessary to provide its core functionalities. Depending on how the User interacts with the Application, the following categories of personal data may be collected:

- Account Data: Email address and, where applicable, name and profile information provided through Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple at the time of registration. With Sign in with Apple, the email may be a relay address provided by Apple.

- Profile and Preferences: Display name, avatar, skill level, personal goals, session type preferences, language, theme, and dashboard configuration set by the User within the Application. Certain profile information, including display name, avatar, skill level, statistics, achievements, and rankings, may be visible to other users of the Application when the User shares their profile via QR code.

- Gym Data: Name, location, coordinates, type, and difficulty levels of climbing gyms associated with the User's account.

- Support Data: Email address and content of messages sent voluntarily by the User to RhumbLabs support.

- Legal Acceptances: Record of the version of the Terms and Privacy Policy accepted by the User, including date and time of acceptance.

- Session Data: Climbing session records entered by the User, including routes, difficulty levels, gym visited, dates, and personal notes.

- Shared Session Data: When a User voluntarily creates or joins a Shared Session, their display name, avatar, logged route levels, fall count, score, and activity timestamps are temporarily shared with the other participants in that room. The complete session is saved only in each participant's individual history. Temporary rooms expire after 12 hours and are automatically removed from Firebase's active systems.

- Location Data: Geographic coordinates of the device, read only when the User has granted location permission. They are used on the device for two purposes: (i) to identify nearby climbing gyms within a 200-meter radius, and (ii) to determine the country whose climbing events are shown in News. Coordinates are not stored permanently and are not sent to RhumbLabs servers; for News, only the resulting two-letter country code is kept (see News Data). If the location does not determine it, the country is taken, on the device, from the country of the mobile network or SIM card, from the device's time zone or, failing those, from the region set on the device; the User can always choose it manually.

- Community Data (optional): If the User turns on Community, the Application processes their alias, Instagram username, bio, city, gym, roles (climber, setter or gym), private-account setting, the people they follow and who follow them, blocks, the session and achievement posts they choose to share, reactions, and the reports they send or receive. The Community profile, posts and follower counts are visible to other users according to the User's privacy settings. Blocks and reports are not visible to the people involved.

- Direct Messages: The content of the messages the User sends and receives, their participants and read status. Messages may include photos, which are stored in Cloud Storage and can be seen only by the two participants. Messages are visible only to the two participants. RhumbLabs may review a message, including its photo, when it has been reported, solely for moderation.

- Gym Pages: Gym accounts may publish an address, opening hours, website, events, setter team, and cover and logo photos. This information is public to users of the Application.

- Push Notifications: A device identifier for notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging token), platform, language, time zone and notification preferences, including muted gyms and the weekly events summary. They are used only to send alerts that the User can turn off in Settings or in the operating system.

- News Data: The two-letter code of the country whose climbing events News shows, stored with the User's notification preferences so that the optional weekly summary (sent on Sunday morning, local time) lists the events of that country. Which announcements the User dismissed, when News was last opened and a manually chosen country are stored only on the device. Announcements and curated events are published by RhumbLabs; gym events are published by gym accounts and are linked to the country of the gym.

- Images: The Application accesses the photo library only when a gym account picks a cover or logo photo, or when the User picks a photo to send in a chat. A cover or logo photo is uploaded to Cloud Storage and is public within the Application; a chat photo is visible only to the two participants.

- Camera Data: The device camera is accessed to scan QR codes, to allow the User to view profiles or voluntarily join Shared Sessions, and to take a photo when the User chooses to send one in a chat. Camera images used to scan QR codes are not stored or transmitted; a photo taken for a chat is sent only if the User confirms it.

- Usage and Technical Data: Information about how the User interacts with the Application, crash reports, and technical diagnostics, collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

- Advertising Data: If the User is on the free version of the Application, Google AdMob may collect data related to ad interactions and device identifiers in accordance with Google's advertising policies.

## 2. How We Collect Data

Data is collected through the following means:

- Directly from the User, when registering an account, logging climbing sessions, or interacting with Application features.

- Automatically, through Firebase services integrated into the Application, which collect technical and usage data during normal use.

- Through third-party authentication, when the User chooses to sign in via Google or Apple.

## 3. Purpose of Data Processing

The personal data collected is used exclusively for the following purposes:

- To create and manage the User's account.

- To enable the core features of the Application, including session logging, real-time Shared Sessions, gym proximity detection, and profile sharing via QR code.

- To synchronize, back up, and protect User data stored in the Application.

- To analyze usage patterns and improve Application performance and user experience.

- To detect and resolve technical errors and crashes.

- To display personalized or contextual advertising in the free version of the Application.

- To process and validate the one-time in-app purchase for the ad-free version.

- To enable the optional social features: Community profile, posts, messages, gym pages and events.

- To send push notifications that the User can turn off, including an optional weekly summary of climbing events in the User's country.

- To show News: Pogo update announcements and climbing events in the User's country.

- To moderate content and handle reports in order to keep the community safe.

- To respond to User support requests and inquiries.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

Personal data is processed for the following purposes under the applicable grounds provided by current Chilean law. Law N° 21.719 will expand the applicable framework from December 1, 2026:

- Consent: The User actively accepts these terms upon first use of the Application. Location and camera access require explicit permission granted by the User through the device's operating system.

- Contractual necessity: Processing is necessary to provide the services described in the Terms and Conditions of Use.

- Legitimate interest: Technical data and crash reports are processed to maintain the security and proper functioning of the Application.

- Legal obligation: Where required by applicable Chilean law. Users may withdraw their consent at any time by contacting RhumbLabs at support@rhumblabs.com or by revoking the relevant device permissions through their device settings. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to its withdrawal. Withdrawal of consent for optional features, such as location access, usage analytics, crash reporting, or personalized advertising, will not prevent the User from accessing the core functionalities of the Application. Only those features that strictly depend on the revoked permission will be affected.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Data Processors

The Application uses the following third-party services, which may process personal data on behalf of RhumbLabs. Each service operates under its own privacy policy:

- Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): Manages user login and identity verification.

- Firebase Cloud Firestore (Google LLC): Stores User session data and Application content in the cloud.

- Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): Collects usage and behavioral data, which may include pseudonymous identifiers, to help improve the Application.

- Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): Collects crash and error reports to detect and fix technical issues.

- Firebase App Check (Google LLC): Verifies the authenticity of requests made to the Application's backend.

- Firebase Remote Config (Google LLC): Allows remote configuration of Application features without requiring an update.

- Cloud Functions for Firebase (Google LLC): Processes Community actions (follows, posts, messages and reports) and account deletion on the server.

- Cloud Storage for Firebase (Google LLC): Stores gym page photos and photos sent in chats.

- Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google LLC): Delivers push notifications.

- Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc.): Authenticates users who choose to sign in with Apple.

- Google AdMob (Google LLC): Manages advertising in the free version of the Application. All Firebase services are operated by Google LLC, headquartered in the United States. Users may consult Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Advertising and Personalization

The free version of the Application may display personalized or non-personalized advertisements through Google AdMob. Personalized ads may be based on the User's previous behavior and interests. Non-personalized ads do not use behavioral history but may still use contextual information, approximate location, device identifiers, and technical data for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregate measurement, and fraud prevention.

Users may manage their advertising preferences through their device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; Android: Settings > Google > Ads). RhumbLabs does not sell personal data.

If new advertising partners or advanced personalization features are incorporated, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## 7. International Data Transfers

Some of the third-party services listed above may process personal data on servers located outside of Chile, including in the United States. Where such transfers occur, they are carried out in accordance with the data protection standards required by Chilean law and subject to the terms and data processing agreements established with each service provider.

## 8. Data Retention

Personal data is retained for as long as the User's account remains active. Upon account deletion, personal data will be deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by applicable law or for legitimate security purposes.

Session data and account information stored through Firebase Cloud Firestore will be deleted in accordance with the data deletion process initiated upon account termination. Deleting the account also deletes the Community profile, posts, reactions, follow and block relationships, conversations (including the other person's messages and photos in those conversations), reports, the gym page with its photos and events, and notification tokens. Data files exported or backed up by the User from within the Application remain under the User's own control.

RhumbLabs is not responsible for the storage, sharing, or use of files that the User saves or manages outside of the Application.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13. RhumbLabs does not knowingly collect personal data from users under 13 years of age. If the Company becomes aware that a user under 13 has created an account, the account and associated data will be deleted without prior notice.
